Garden Vibe AI — Hero Image
===========================

Place your licensed garden lifestyle photo here:

  public/images/hero-garden.jpg

Recommended specs:
  - Dimensions: 1400 × 800 px or larger
  - Aspect ratio: landscape (wider than tall)
  - Format: JPG or WebP
  - Subject: UK garden lifestyle — patio, furniture, plants, stone paving
  - Style: warm, natural light, premium lifestyle

The hero component loads this image via:
  backgroundImage: url('/images/hero-garden.jpg')

If the file is missing, the hero shows an earthy garden-toned gradient
placeholder (no cartoon illustration).
